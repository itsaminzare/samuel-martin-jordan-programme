Samuel Martin Jordan Programme

A non-profit initiative working to expand access to quality education for underserved student communities in Iran.

About the Programme

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity is not.

Since January 2024 the Programme has given 60 underserved students in Iran access to education, and two cohorts have graduated.

The Programme was co-founded in Tehran by a small group of students who wanted others to have the same chance at a good education that they had.

We work with students whose circumstances would otherwise put that education out of reach. Every pound we receive is budgeted, tracked and reported back to the donors who gave it.

What we believe

Seek knowledge from the cradle to the grave.

A Persian saying every Iranian child knows. We believe everyone should get the chance to live by it, whatever their circumstances.

How it works

Learning together, from first term to graduation

Students join the Programme in cohorts and stay with it through to graduation, so no student goes through it alone.

  • Open to students in Iran whose circumstances limit their access to education
  • Taught and supported in cohorts
  • Free to every student we accept

Stewardship

Every rial accounted for

Donors trust us with their money, so we treat each budget line as a promise. We estimate what the Programme will need, set spending priorities in advance, and report back on exactly where the money went.

  • A budget equivalent to about £28,000 managed over 20 months with no overspend
  • 11 financial reports shared with donors
